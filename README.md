# Commercial Insurance Contingent Commission Audit

Calculate contingent commissions from production, retention, and loss performance and recover missed carrier payments.

**Primary buyer:** Insurance agencies, brokers, and carriers. **Evidence:** producer agreements, policies, written premium, earned premium, retention, loss ratios, growth measures, commission statements, and payments.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Producer agreement library
- Policy premium ingestion
- Written premium calculation
- Earned premium calculation
- Growth target validation
- Retention ratio calculation
- Loss ratio calculation
- Profitability corridor
- Tier and hurdle calculation
- Acquisition exclusion control
- Commission statement audit
- Carrier claim package
- Payment reconciliation
- Producer allocation
- Carrier segment analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4661>. API: `5661`.
